Star schema

One central fact table (one row per project cost transaction) joined directly to seven dimensions, with no snowflaking. GL postings and approved budgets are the book of record for totals. Purchase-order and timesheet lines add supplier, material, delivery and hours detail.

Table row counts

Why a star schema?

  • Simple joins: every KPI is one join from the fact table, so queries are fast and easy to explain.
  • Conformed dimensions: project, supplier, material, employee, client, cost category and date are shared by every measure.
  • History kept: project manager/status and employee role changes are tracked as Slowly Changing Dimensions (Type 2).
  • Budget vs actual in one place: a scenario column (ACTUAL / BUDGET) puts both on the same grain.
  • Unknown members (key 0): every foreign key is filled, so no rows are lost in joins.
